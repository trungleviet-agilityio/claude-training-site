Privacy

This page explains what Claude Training saves about you.

What we save

  • Your name and email, from your Google account or your sign-up form.
  • The boxes you tick and the sessions you mark as complete.
  • The date you last saved progress.

Your theme choice (light or dark) stays in your browser only. We do not receive it.

We do not use analytics or advertising on this site.

Pages with diagrams load a drawing tool (Mermaid) from jsDelivr, a public file host. Like any website, it sees your IP address. We send it no other data.

Why we save it

  • To let you continue on any device.
  • To let your mentor see your progress.

Only you and your mentor can read your progress. Your mentor adds your email to the class list.

Where it is stored

We use Firebase, a Google service.

  • Sign-in: Firebase Authentication runs in the United States.
  • Progress: Firestore stores it in Singapore.
  • Logs: Firebase keeps IP addresses in its sign-in logs for a few weeks.

How long we keep it

We delete class list entries and progress when the course ends. After an account is deleted, Firebase can take up to 180 days to remove it from its backups.

Delete your data

Sign in, then press the button below. You can also ask your mentor to delete it.
